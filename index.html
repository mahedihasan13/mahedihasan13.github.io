Thesis Research

2026 – Ongoing

M.Sc. Thesis Ongoing

The Utilization of Alternative Pozzolanic Binders as Supplementary Cementitious Materials · IUT
  • Characterize three starting materials (fine river sand, calcined clay, brick powder) for density, grading, oxide composition (XRF), mineralogy, and amorphous fraction, to see how material type and source affect reactivity.
  • Measure mortar compressive strength at 3, 7, 28 and 90 days for 0–20% cement replacement, and evaluate the strength activity index (SAI) against the ASTM C618 limit of 75%.
  • Explain the strength results through XRD, TGA (portlandite consumption) and SEM to establish each binder's pozzolanic reaction mechanism.
