About

About me.

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Fareast International University and an M.Sc. researcher in Civil Engineering at the Islamic University of Technology. My work focuses on sustainable construction materials and concrete technology, with particular interest in supplementary cementitious materials and low-carbon cementitious systems.

I also study the seismic performance and resilience of reinforced concrete structures, using computational and numerical modeling to understand how material behavior influences structural response.

Research

My research.

M.Sc. Thesis · Ongoing

The Utilization of Alternative Pozzolanic Binders as Supplementary Cementitious Materials

Islamic University of Technology (IUT). The thesis studies fine river sand, calcined clay and brick powder as partial cement replacements and explains their performance through their mineralogy and reaction mechanisms.

Research Focus

Supplementary Cementitious Materials Pozzolanic Reactivity Low-Carbon Concrete Material Characterization

Objectives

  1. Characterize the starting materials (XRF, mineralogy, amorphous fraction) to see how type and source affect reactivity.
  2. Measure mortar strength at 3, 7, 28 and 90 days for 0–20% replacement and evaluate the strength activity index against ASTM C618.
  3. Explain the results through XRD, TGA and SEM.

Sustainable Construction Materials

Supplementary cementitious materials, natural pozzolans and low-carbon cementitious systems to reduce the carbon footprint of concrete.

Concrete Technology

Mineralogy, pozzolanic reactivity, hydration behavior, and the link between material properties and mechanical performance.

Seismic Resilience

Seismic performance, analysis and resilience of reinforced concrete structures.

Computational Modeling

Numerical analysis of material behavior and its influence on structural response.
